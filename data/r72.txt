### Positive reading (I-POS)
- **What it means:** The low-response zones are open fractures. Storage in the 120-250 m window is the largest of the three readings (P50 10,805 m3) and a pathway from the upper zone reaches the well in 97-100 % of realisations across the candidate sites.
- **What to do:** Drill the priority site (A) first; log fractures and water strikes between 100 and 300 m; keep the recharge package because the demand still draws the aquifer down: S6 drawdown P50 1.5 m with an open boundary (low (< 5 m)) and 2.9 m with a closed boundary (low (< 5 m)).
- **Boundary:** Open boundary: drawdown P50 1.5 m, recharge retained 1 %. Closed boundary: drawdown P50 2.9 m, recharge retained 100 %. With a closed boundary the site lives on its own recharge, so the recharge package matters more.
- **Borehole evidence that confirms it:** open fractures with water strikes in at least two of the predicted intervals (P2); yield step test holds

### Neutral reading (I-NEU)
- **What it means:** Only the strongest low-response class (C1) is fractured. Fractures are patchy: storage P50 6,296 m3 and a pathway from the upper zone in 20-34 % of realisations - a borewell may hit a fracture or miss it.
- **What to do:** Drill to the logged fracture, not to a fixed depth; keep the reserve site ready; S6 drawdown P50 1.7 m with an open boundary (low (< 5 m)) and 3.1 m with a closed boundary (low (< 5 m)); recharge must be placed where the borehole proves a connected fracture.
- **Boundary:** Open boundary: drawdown P50 1.7 m, recharge retained 1 %. Closed boundary: drawdown P50 3.1 m, recharge retained 100 %. With a closed boundary the site lives on its own recharge, so the recharge package matters more.
- **Borehole evidence that confirms it:** one fractured interval near the predicted depth; other low-response intervals dry or tight

### Negative reading (I-NEG)
- **What it means:** No ADMT class carries water; C1 is clay/gouge. Storage is the smallest (P50 4,806 m3) and the site depends on weathered-zone water and on recharge.
- **What to do:** Reduce the borewell demand, rely first on roof water, tanks and biopools; S6 drawdown P50 5.2 m with an open boundary (moderate (5-20 m)) and 4.7 m with a closed boundary (low (< 5 m)); a single borewell should be treated as a test well, not a supply.
- **Boundary:** Open boundary: drawdown P50 5.2 m, recharge retained 1 %. Closed boundary: drawdown P50 4.7 m, recharge retained 100 %. With a closed boundary the site lives on its own recharge, so the recharge package matters more.
- **Borehole evidence that confirms it:** low-response intervals logged as clay/gouge or tight rock; no strike in 100-300 m

### Robust actions
Actions that hold in all three readings: (1) build the roof-water capture and storage first; (2) drill the priority site as an exploratory borewell with full logging and a pumping test; (3) measure static water level and install a water-level logger; (4) decide the deep-recharge design only after the borehole shows which reading survives.
